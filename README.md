# Nova Peptide Research Dashboard

Purchases, sales, inventory and financials for Nova Peptide Research Canada.

- The app is in this repository and is published with GitHub Pages.
- Your records are stored in your own Firebase project (Firestore), and only your Google account can read them.
- The app keeps a copy on your phone, so it works offline and syncs when you're back online.
- Opening the app never changes your data. It only saves when you add, edit or delete something.

To connect the app to Firebase, paste your settings into `firebase-config.js`.

To update the app later, upload the new files over the old ones.
